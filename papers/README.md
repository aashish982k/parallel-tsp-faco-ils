# Base research paper

Rafał Skinderowicz (2022). *Improving Ant Colony Optimization Efficiency for
Solving Large TSP Instances*. Applied Soft Computing, article 108653.

- [Attached accepted manuscript](FACO_base_research_paper.pdf)
- [Original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02228)
- [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asoc.2022.108653)

The PDF is the unmodified author's accepted manuscript, distributed under its
stated Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 license.
Copyright and license notices remain in the PDF. It is included for this academic
project's research attribution.

The accompanying ILS implementation draws on Lourenço, Martin and Stützle,
[Iterated Local Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0102188).
